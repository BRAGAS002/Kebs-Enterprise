About KEBS Enterprise

Your trusted printing partner since 1996

Our History

Since its establishment in 1996, KEBS Enterprise has been a cornerstone in the printing industry. We began with mimeograph printing, T-shirt printing, photocopying, and bookbinding services, including hardbound, spiral, perfect binding, and morocco soft binding.

Over the years, we've expanded our operations by incorporating advanced printing machines and embracing modern printing techniques. By the early 2000s, we had developed a loyal customer base, enabling us to invest in cutting-edge equipment and broaden our service offerings.

From 2010 onward, we've remained committed to continuous learning and technological advancements, ensuring the delivery of high-quality printing solutions to meet customer expectations.

Our Business

Operating as a sole proprietorship, KEBS Enterprise benefits from a flexible business model that enables quick decision-making and a personalized approach to customer service. We are located at #36 A. Bonifacio Street, Baguio City, across Caltex Gas Station.
